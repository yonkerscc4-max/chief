## The headline

Sales are booming and each guest is spending less. Net sales nearly doubled
Feb→Jun while spend per guest fell 22% — six straight months of decline.
Volume is not the problem, **structure is**. Every move below raises spend per
guest without prices guests will feel and without depending on server training.

## What the data says

1. **Tuesday is losing money — it's math.** Lowest guests, lowest check, labor
   34.1% vs Friday's 16.5%; prime cost near 65%. Staffing to Wednesday levels
   saves ~$22K/yr before the programming question. Salsa night earns its slot
   or gets retested.
2. **Sunday is the biggest untapped day.** Best food day ($10.96/guest),
   weakest weekend bar ($11.06), $629 in brunch cocktails all quarter. A
   printed bottomless package is the most proven check-lifter; 25% uptake
   lifts Sunday $3–4/guest.
3. **The server gap is real:** $22.01–$29.94 per guest among 20+ shift servers
   (36% spread on the same menu). Top two: Joe Rubbo and Chrissy Heffelfinger
   near $30 — Chrissy at highest volume in the house. Debrief them; print what
   they say into menus, bundles, and prompts. Structure over training.
4. **Voids: $12.6K/quarter (~$50K/yr) with no reason codes.** Tracks volume,
   not misconduct — reads as error and workflow. Reason codes + manager PIN
   over $15 + weekly report.
5. **~16% of sales flow through the unnamed "Online Ordering" center**
   ($32.7K in June) — likely QR at the table. Confirm routing; it's the best
   place to install upsell prompts that never miss a shift.
6. **Beer: the brand won, push the margin.** Not Upstate Hazy and Pilsner are
   #1–2; but Corona + Coors Light took 1,600+ pours at 25–30 pts worse margin.
   House beers listed first everywhere. Flights ($4.8K) and six-packs ($7.2K)
   quietly working.
7. **Dead categories are free money:** desserts $2,492/90d (live on the drink
   menu), NA $0.32/guest (no NA beer, no mocktails), merch $1,115/quarter,
   off-premise 4.6% (skip the apps; grow direct takeout bundles).
8. **Reporting measures a blurry business:** $4,222 uncategorized Margarita,
   Wing Night uncategorized, duplicate beer buttons, no revenue centers. One
   afternoon of Toast admin fixes it permanently. Discounts/comps are clean
   (2.4%, normal band — marketing, not leakage).
