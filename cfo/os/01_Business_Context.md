# 01 — Business Context

**Yonkers Brewing Company LLC** — craft brewery, taproom/restaurant, and live
music venue in Yonkers, NY. Owner: John Rubbo. Leadership: John, AJ, Ian,
Maria, Paul.

## The one idea that explains everything

**YBC is two businesses in one building:**

- A **restaurant** Tuesday–Thursday and Sunday
- A **music venue** Friday and Saturday nights

Friday hourly data proves it: food is 64% of the mix at noon, 30% by 8pm, near
zero after 10pm while the bar keeps pouring. The venue model works as designed.
The growth plan targets the restaurant days and the brunch window and leaves
venue nights alone.

## Revenue structure in QuickBooks

- **Wholesale** = 4100s (packaged 4/6/12, kegs 1/6 bbl, kegs 1/2 bbl)
- **Retail** = 4200s, menu-categorized (Beer, Liquor, Wine, Small Plates, Big
  Plates, Sandwiches, Brunch, Desserts, Non-Alcoholic, …)
- **Live Music & Events** = 9101
- COGS mirrors revenue: Food (5210), Beer internal (5215), Beer external
  (5220), Wine/Liquor (5230), Wholesale (5100)

This channel separation is a strength — it's the structure a buyer needs to
value each stream, and what the playbooks run on.
